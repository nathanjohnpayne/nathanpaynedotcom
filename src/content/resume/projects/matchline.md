A career CRM for a serious job search: it turns work history into structured, reusable evidence, maps that evidence against specific job requirements, and generates applications whose every claim is checked against approved evidence before it can be exported. Development stopped before launch after extraction accuracy reached 51.3% against an 80% quality bar; the final work repaired a noisy evaluation system rather than improving the product.
