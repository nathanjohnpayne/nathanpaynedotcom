A career CRM for a serious job search: it turns work history into structured, reusable evidence, maps that evidence to specific job requirements, and generates applications whose every claim must trace to approved evidence—a gate that was built but never validated with users, in front of an export that remained a stub. Paused before launch at 48% extraction and 19% match against an 80% bar set before any measurement. On one test pair, two scorer changes moved extraction 47 points; a product change moved it one.
