Every project started as a real problem. Each one became a systems design exercise.

Device Source of Truth Enterprise × Data

Disney has hundreds of partner devices across Disney+, Hulu, and ESPN—each with different hardware specs, DRM requirements, and codec support tracked across Airtable, Datadog, and spreadsheets. DST consolidates all of it into a single React + Firebase app with rules-based device scoring. Built for the partner engineering world I live in. Live ↗

Override Finance × Theater

The financial operating system for Broadway productions. Structure your capitalization, model investor returns, manage ownership, and share live deals with backers—without spreadsheets or PDF workflows. Live ↗

Swipe Watch Consumer × Streaming

A swipe-based discovery experiment for Disney+ and Hulu. Train your recommendations, earn coins, and build your next watchlist—built in vanilla JS over a weekend. Live ↗

Friends & Family Billing Utility × Finance

Cloud-synced shared-bill coordination for families and friend groups, turning monthly and annual costs into clear annual invoices, payment tracking, and shareable summaries. Live ↗
