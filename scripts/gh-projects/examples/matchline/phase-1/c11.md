The Phase 1 exit criterion: end-to-end QA that the core loop works on real data.

**Exit bar** (from PRD § What the V1 experience is):

> The first session success criterion: the user generates one application they would actually send, in under fifteen minutes, without editing any fabricated claims out.

Scaled to the milestone: the loop works on at least three distinct real-world JDs in Nathan's target market (streaming video infrastructure, developer tools, AI-augmented workflows).

**Scope:**

1. **Run the loop.** For each of three real JDs:
   - Paste Nathan's real resume → review Units → approve.
   - Paste the JD → review Requirements → confirm matches → approve.
   - Generate the resume → walk the validation flags → resolve / edit.
   - Confirm the final resume is one Nathan would send.
2. **Measure.** Record:
   - Total time from paste-resume to validated export.
   - Per-stage LLM cost.
   - Number of flags raised by validation, and the resolution path for each.
   - Zero-fabrication check: walk every claim in the final resume and confirm it traces.
3. **Log issues.** Anything that broke, felt wrong, or violated the spec gets a sub-issue under the Phase 3 Bug Triage umbrella — not this ticket.
4. **Sign-off.** Nathan is the sole reviewer.

**Exit criteria** (all must pass):
- Full flow (paste resume → paste JD → validated export) p95 ≤ 20 s over the three runs.
- Per-application LLM cost ≤ $1 across the three runs (target $0.75).
- Zero fabrication: zero un-sourced claims in any final resume. Not "rare" — zero.
- Nathan signs off that each of the three outputs is something he'd send.

If any exit criterion fails, the phase doesn't close. The failing condition becomes a new sub-issue under the relevant Phase 1 or Phase 3 parent.
