Take the Application Editor from Phase 1 #c9 ("works") to "usable for a real application without friction."

**Scope:**

1. **Hover-to-highlight.** Hovering a bullet on the left pane highlights the contributing Unit(s) on the right, with a light color that matches the bullet's accent. Bi-directional: hovering a Unit on the right highlights every bullet that used it.

2. **Edit preserves traceability where possible.** When the user edits a bullet, diff the old and new text at the claim level. Claims still supported by the original Units stay traced; new claims trigger re-validation and may raise new flags. Already spec'd in Phase 1 #c9 — this ticket is where the edge cases land (partial edits, reorders, cut/paste between bullets).

3. **Bulk-approve in Unit Review.** Ship a bulk-select + approve flow in Unit Review — Phase 1 ships one-at-a-time; this phase makes onboarding 100+ Units not feel like a chore.

4. **Quick-add Unit from flag.** The "resolve by adding a supporting Unit" path from Phase 1 #c9 opens a modal; this ticket makes the modal pre-fill `raw_text` from the bullet and suggest `skills`/`tools`/`domains` from the bullet's context.

5. **Version history, lightweight.** Every `Generate` click saves a new AssetRef rather than overwriting. Surface "previous versions" in a collapsed panel. Not a full diff; just restore-point links.

**Non-goals:**
- Collaborative editing — V1 is single-user.
- Rich-text formatting beyond plain text + basic structure — export formats handle the rendering.

**Verification:**
- Playwright E2E: edit → re-validate → flag-resolve → export.
- Manual: bulk-approve 40 Units in under 60 s.
- Manual: restore a previous version, confirm it renders with original validation flags.
